## How to contact

For ordinary product support, open an issue at https://github.com/intronode/holdpoint-docs/issues. Include the site hostname (for example `your-site.atlassian.net`), the work item key, the Holdpoint state shown in the panel, and what you expected to happen. Do not include passwords, API tokens, or the full text of a supplier message unless you need it to explain the problem. Account ids are enough when a name is not required. Do not put personal data in a public issue. Privacy requests and security reports do not use this tracker.

## What you can expect

We aim to reply within five business days. A business day is Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays in the Republic of Korea.

That sentence is the whole support promise. It does not cover weekends. It is not an uptime guarantee and not a promise that a Jira or Forge incident will be resolved in that time.

This page is the support Intronode offers for this free app.

## Security reports

Report a security issue in private. Use the repository's **Security → Report a vulnerability** form (GitHub private vulnerability reporting), or write to privacy@intronode.com. Do not open a public issue.

We aim to reply within five business days, on the same business-day rule. That is the same promise as ordinary support, and nothing stronger.

## What this contact channel is not

- It is not a way to approve a Holdpoint proposal. Approval happens in the Holdpoint panel in Jira.
- It is not an emergency payment desk.
- It does not cover a customer's Rovo or MCP bill. Those are the customer's Atlassian and client accounts.
